Our Story

Bridging Rural Roots with Urban Trust

SHIKOR was born out of a desire to connect Bangladesh's rich agricultural heritage and artisanal crafts directly with urban households. By managing the entire sourcing, packaging, and logistics process, we eliminate multi-vendor variations and secure high standards of purity.

Your support directly empowers micro-entrepreneurs and farming families in rural regions, ensuring sustainable incomes and keeping traditional craftsmanship alive.
