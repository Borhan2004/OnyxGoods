Why Our Milk Is Different

Naturally Rich, Safely Chilled & Untouched

Most commercial milk spends days traveling through middlemen, getting chemically treated or mixed in giant silos. We believe in preserving the traditional taste of pure village cow milk.

  • Collected from verified village farmer families caring for native cows.
  • Chilled immediately at village collection centers to halt bacteria growth.
  • Rigorous quality verification for fat, purity, and water before dispatch.
  • Transported 250 KM in specialized cold chains (under 4°C).
  • Delivered fresh to your doorstep within 24 hours of cow milking—no middlemen.
